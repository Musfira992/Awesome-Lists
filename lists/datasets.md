# Awesome datasets

Open omics, health, and plant datasets useful for learning and research. Short blurb plus URL for each.

## Gene and genome

- [NCBI Gene (example: CFTR)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/1080) - Reference gene records; CFTR encodes an ABC transporter implicated in cystic fibrosis.
- [Ensembl](https://www.ensembl.org/) - Annotated genomes for vertebrates and selected other species, with BioMart and REST APIs.
- [NCBI RefSeq](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/refseq/) - Curated non-redundant nucleotide and protein sequences.

## Sequencing archives

- [NCBI GEO](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/) - Gene Expression Omnibus; microarray and high-throughput functional genomics data.
- [NCBI SRA](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sra) - Sequence Read Archive; raw NGS reads linked to BioProjects and BioSamples.
- [EBI ArrayExpress](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/arrayexpress) - Functional genomics experiments complementary to GEO.
- [EBI ENA](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ena) - European Nucleotide Archive; reads, assemblies, and annotations.

## Plant and agriculture

- [Phytozome](https://phytozome-next.jgi.doe.gov/) - Plant comparative genomics from JGI; genomes, gene families, and downloads.
- [TAIR (Arabidopsis)](https://www.arabidopsis.org/) - Arabidopsis thaliana genome, genes, and community resources.
- [Gramene](https://www.gramene.org/) - Comparative plant genomics with a focus on crops.
- [Crop Ontology](https://cropontology.org/) - Controlled vocabularies for crop traits and phenotyping.

## Health and clinical

- [MIMIC-IV](https://physionet.org/content/mimiciv/3.1/) - Large de-identified ICU EHR database for critical care research (credentialed access).
- [PhysioNet](https://physionet.org/) - Open physiological signal and clinical datasets beyond MIMIC.

## Teaching and miscellaneous

- [Jeff Leek teaching data (Fred Hutch / jtleek)](https://github.com/jtleek/data) - Small teaching datasets used in data science courses.
- [DIY Transcriptomics materials](https://diytranscriptomics.com/) - Course site with accompanying data folders for hands-on RNA-seq teaching.
- [GTEx Portal](https://gtexportal.org/) - Genotype-Tissue Expression project; multi-tissue human expression and eQTL data.
- [1000 Genomes / IGSR](https://www.internationalgenome.org/) - Human genetic variation from global populations.
