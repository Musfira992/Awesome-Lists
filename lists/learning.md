# Learning resources

Courses, books, and structured paths for research computing, bioinformatics, and data skills.

## Courses and interactive

- [The Missing Semester of Your CS Education](https://missing.csail.mit.edu/) - MIT course on the shell, editors, Git, debugging, and practical computing that most degrees skip.
- [DIY Transcriptomics](https://diytranscriptomics.com/) - Hands-on RNA-seq course materials from Stephen Turner and colleagues.
- [HarvardX: Data Analysis for Life Sciences](https://www.edx.org/learn/data-analysis/harvard-university-data-analysis-for-life-sciences) - Rafael Irizarry series covering R, stats, and genomics.
- [Coursera Genomic Data Science (Johns Hopkins)](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/genomic-data-science) - Pipeline-oriented intro spanning command line, Python/R, and Bioconductor.
- [Software Carpentry](https://software-carpentry.org/) - Community lessons on the Unix shell, Git, Python, and R for researchers.
- [Data Carpentry](https://datacarpentry.org/) - Domain-focused data skills workshops (including genomics and ecology).

## Books and long-form

- [Modern Statistics for Modern Biology (Holmes & Huber)](https://web.stanford.edu/class/bios221/book/) - Free online book linking multivariate stats to biological data.
- [R for Data Science (Wickham, Çetinkaya-Rundel, Grolemund)](https://r4ds.hadley.nz/) - Practical tidyverse workflow from import to communication.
- [Happy Git and GitHub for the useR (Jenny Bryan)](https://happygitwithr.com/) - Git/GitHub aimed at R users and researchers.
- [Bioconductor workflows](https://www.bioconductor.org/packages/release/BiocViews.html#___Workflow) - Official end-to-end analysis vignettes.

## Reference hubs

- [Musfira notes handbook](https://www.musfirajamil.com/notes/) - Technical notes for research and ops practice.
- [bioinformatics-resources](https://github.com/Musfira992/bioinformatics-resources) - Curated tools and references companion to this list.
